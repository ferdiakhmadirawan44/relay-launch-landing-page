WHY RELAY

EVERYTHING FEEDBACK NEEDS, NOTHING IT DOESN’T

Six tools that replace the spreadsheet, the shared inbox and the quarterly guessing game.

[AI triage]

AI triage, in seconds

Relay reads every new message, tags the topic, scores the sentiment and groups duplicates — so a thousand voices become ten clear themes.

[Voting]

Roadmap voting that counts

Customers vote on what matters to them, and every vote is weighted by plan, usage and churn risk — not just whoever shouts loudest.

[Changelog]

A changelog that writes itself

Ship a feature and Relay drafts the announcement from the closed feedback, ready to send to everyone who asked for it.

[Integrations]

Plugs into your stack

Native two-way sync with Slack, Intercom, Zendesk, HubSpot, Jira and Linear. Feedback flows in; status flows back out.

[Sentiment]

Sentiment you can track

Watch satisfaction drift by segment, plan or feature area, and get alerted the moment a key account turns unhappy.

[Alerts]

Alerts before churn

When a high-value account files three complaints in a week, Relay pings the owner in Slack — before the renewal call, not after it.

HOW IT WORKS

FROM INBOX CHAOS TO ROADMAP IN THREE STEPS

01

Connect your channels

Plug in email, chat, your help desk and social inboxes. Relay starts importing history immediately — no migration project.

02

Let AI sort the noise

Duplicates merge, topics cluster and sentiment scores land automatically. You review themes, not a thousand tickets.

03
